Legal AI Chatbot by AIMP LABS
At AIMP LABS, innovation is at the heart of everything we do. This section showcases the cutting-edge technologies and novel solutions developed by our team. From AI-powered visual systems to large-scale machine learning frameworks, we continuously push the boundaries of what's possible—delivering practical, impactful applications that drive real-world value.

Recently We have developed an advanced AI-powered chatbot designed to interact with and interpret the three recently enacted Indian laws:
  • BNS (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita) - Replacing the Indian Penal Code (IPC), it governs criminal offenses.
  • BNSS (Bharatiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita) - Replacing the Criminal Procedure Code (CrPC), it outlines procedures for criminal investigations and trials.
  • BSA (Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam) - Replacing the Indian Evidence Act, it governs the rules of evidence in legal proceedings.
    • Disclaimer: This chatbot provides general information and is not a substitute for professional legal advice. For specific legal issues, please consult a qualified attorney.
